# Selected BFSI challenge and product fit

User-confirmed official challenge wording, 17 October 2026.

## BFSI: Intelligent Risk, Fraud & Financial Experiences

> Build an AI-powered solution that addresses meaningful challenges across banking, financial services, insurance, or related industries. Solutions may focus on detecting and mitigating fraud or risk, automating complex processes, improving customer interactions, or delivering more personalized and informed financial experiences.

MoveBooks AI addresses accounting migration risk, automates a controlled complex workflow and supports informed financial decisions. Its bounded synthetic demonstration connects evidence-backed mapping, human consequential approval, deterministic recovery/reconciliation and verified first productive use. Gemini explains uncertainty; the independent Reviewer challenges unsupported mapping assumptions. This is a financial-process and risk-reduction fit. No fraud-detection, insurance-underwriting or real-provider capability is claimed.

The challenge statement establishes thematic alignment. It does not establish eligibility, scoring, production readiness or expected awards. Candidate evidence and current activation boundaries remain authoritative.
