# Working Backwards decision summary

Version 1, 17 October 2026. Decision reference for implementation, not release authorization. Approval status: reviewable product draft.

## Commitments

1. Deliver three distinct entries: Play, Explore Demo and Try Your Data, converging only where a supported canonical handoff exists.
2. Keep the full five-phase story and underlying lifecycle through verified FPU. Do not relabel an import as success.
3. Preserve user-triggered Gemini, a separate independent Reviewer contract and internal Judges outside financial execution.
4. Keep deterministic financial authority, human consequential decisions and owner-scoped evidence.
5. Keep source/target adapters separate and label all scope as bounded synthetic Beta.
6. Treat public upload, live model/cloud controls and deployment as separate release gates.

## Trade-offs and decisions

One Play mission precedes additional content. Browser-local intake fallback retains the customer job while hosted upload safety is unfinished. Deterministic workflows simplify reliable control; no autonomous LLM delegation claim follows from logical agent names. Cloud SQL remains financial authority, while Firestore operational integration cannot be inferred from existing audit records. A hypothetical mature press release can guide acceptance but cannot expand current permissions.

## Risks and unresolved assumptions

Demand, learning transfer, reviewer utility, adapter economics and production cost remain unvalidated hypotheses. Real-data handling, supported accounting semantics, role delegation, retention/deletion and operating response need independent evidence before broader launch. Effective Vertex inference permissions and regional model access require verification. Firestore activation and project-level IAM/configuration mutations require explicit authorization.

## Release decisions

Current target remains a synthetic submission candidate, not production customer migration. A capability can be implemented and still not deployed or accepted. Unknowns remain explicit in the [promise matrix](../product-management/promise-traceability.md). Hosted uploads are No-Go until independent controls and negative tests pass. Live Gemini/Firestore remain gated until their runtime evidence and operational controls pass. No official-template, final-video or portal-compliance claim may be made from a draft asset.

## How this package guides work

Every major promise has a [feature acceptance contract](../product-management/feature-contracts.md), backlog ID, source/test anchor and deployed-evidence field. The Product Quality Judge checks the implemented product against those promises. A failed criterion creates a backlog discrepancy and a release decision; speculative wording never overrules safety. The integration owner reconciles the matrix against the final candidate commit, browser acceptance and actual deployment evidence.
