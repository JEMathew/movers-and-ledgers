# Hypothetical future product press release

**WORKING BACKWARDS FICTION. Intended mature experience. Not an actual availability announcement, customer testimonial, launch commitment or production certification.** Version 1, 17 October 2026. No future date or launch market selected.

## Accountants can move their books and verify their first productive workday

### MoveBooks AI helps businesses understand the move, control consequential decisions and prove the result

Movers & Ledgers imagines introducing MoveBooks AI for accountants, bookkeepers and businesses migrating between explicitly supported accounting environments. The promise is **Move your books. Keep your confidence.** Customers would understand what can move, what needs their decision, how correctness is checked and whether their new working environment supports the first agreed accounting task.

An accountant considering a move faces more than a file transfer. A clearing account may have an unclear purpose. An outstanding invoice may depend on a customer record or tax setting. A technically complete import may still leave the owner unsure whether the books are usable. These are product hypotheses grounded in financial workflow requirements, not a claim that customer research has established their prevalence.

Customers would begin with the experience that suits their confidence. Play makes migration tangible through an accountant's office mission. Explore Demo rehearses the full journey using Harbor Light Books. Try Your Data validates explicitly permitted records against a documented package, explains incompatibilities and asks for review before a supported workspace handoff. Its availability and permitted data would be stated before any upload.

The workspace guides **Understand, Prepare, Move, Verify and Start**. It inventories records, assesses readiness and creates a versioned migration plan. When account 1200, Misc Clearing, has ambiguous treatment, the customer can inspect evidence, request a Gemini explanation and obtain an independent review that challenges unsupported assumptions. Advice and review return to the accountant. The authorized human approves, rejects or modifies consequential decisions.

Approved synthetic or future supported execution would preserve lineage and checkpoints. Policy permits bounded transient recovery and escalates uncertain or consequential failures. Deterministic reconciliation checks the agreed accounting invariants. Financial discrepancies stop progression for investigation. Sensitive configuration requires approval. Completion would require the authorized user to perform the agreed first productive task after all required controls pass, with a verifiable receipt rather than a self-reported success badge.

The intended benefit is a coherent handover: learn the concepts, see the risk, make informed decisions, verify the numbers and begin work. Differentiation depends on continuity across those steps and the separation of reasoning, review, financial execution and human authority. No measured time saving, adoption, accuracy or commercial impact is asserted.

Privacy and safety would remain explicit. Supported adapters would define their scope and permissions. Financial and transactional authority would remain in governed deterministic services and the relational store. Models would receive only permitted projections, never uploaded raw records. Operational controls would enforce budgets and safe stops. The product would explain exclusions, unresolved issues and recovery responsibilities before execution.

### Explicit launch assumptions

The mature experience requires validated provider adapters, reviewed real-data handling, reliable identity and owner isolation, supported financial semantics, retention/deletion, recoverability, cloud controls, sustainable economics and observed user acceptance. It also assumes users value the integrated handover enough to choose the product. These assumptions require evidence, not wording changes.

**Available scope today:** a bounded synthetic Beta reference implementation. No real accounting-provider migration, unrestricted financial-data processing, autonomous authorization or production compliance certification is offered by this release fiction. Hosted uploads, live Gemini, Reviewer deployment and Firestore activation must each satisfy their current evidence and release gates.
