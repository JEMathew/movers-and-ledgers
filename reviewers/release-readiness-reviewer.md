# Release-readiness reviewer

Apply the shared contract in [README](README.md).

Assess all 17 areas in [`docs/RELEASE_READINESS.md`](../docs/RELEASE_READINESS.md) against current evidence. Report GREEN, AMBER, RED, or NOT ASSESSED for each; missing evidence is not GREEN. P0 blocks GREEN, and unresolved P1 normally blocks GREEN unless an authorized, documented exception satisfies the policy. Provide a final decision, blocking findings, accepted risks, rollback/recovery gaps, owners, and re-review conditions.
