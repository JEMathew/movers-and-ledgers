# Release-readiness reviewer

Apply the shared contract in [README](README.md).

Assess all 17 areas in [`docs/RELEASE_READINESS.md`](../docs/RELEASE_READINESS.md) against current evidence. Report GREEN, AMBER, RED, NOT APPLICABLE, or NOT ASSESSED for each; require rationale for NOT APPLICABLE, and do not treat missing evidence as GREEN. P0 blocks release. Any open or accepted P1 blocks GREEN; an accepted P1 remains AMBER under the documented exception policy. Provide a final decision, blocking findings, accepted risks, rollback/recovery gaps, owners, and re-review conditions.
