# Metrics reviewer

Apply the shared contract in [README](README.md).

Review the North Star, customer outcomes, product/business measures, migration quality, agent/model quality, safety/trust, and reliability together. Require formulas, denominators, windows, segments, sources, owners, data-quality and privacy treatment, and guardrails. Distinguish leading/lagging, outcome/health, product/model, and business/operational metrics. Reject invented measurements and unlabelled numeric targets.
